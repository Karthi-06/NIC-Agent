Health Insurance

Motor Insurance

Protect your car or two-wheeler with comprehensive motor insurance. Whether it's an accident, theft, or natural disaster, ride with confidence knowing you're fully covered.

  • Third-party & comprehensive cover
  • Zero depreciation add-on
  • Roadside assistance
  • Own damage protection

Commercial Vehicles

Your business depends on your fleet. Protect every truck, bus, and commercial vehicle with specialised coverage that keeps your operations running no matter what.

  • Fleet & single vehicle coverage
  • Goods in transit protection
  • Driver personal accident cover
04

Fire Insurance

Safeguard your property — whether home or commercial premises — against fire, lightning, explosions, and allied perils. Rebuild without financial ruin.

  • Building & contents coverage
  • Allied perils (floods, storms, etc.)
  • Business interruption cover
  • Reinstatement value basis

Electric Vehicle Insurance

Specialised insurance designed for electric vehicles, covering unique risks like battery damage, charging equipment, and EV-specific repairs at authorised service centres.

  • Battery & charging unit cover
  • EV-specific own damage cover
  • Roadside & charging assistance
  • Zero emission vehicle discounts
06

Marine Insurance

For businesses that rely on shipping — cover your cargo and vessels across coastal and inland waterways against transit risks, loss, and damage.

  • Cargo & freight cover
  • Inland water transport
  • Import & export coverage
  • Hull & machinery insurance
